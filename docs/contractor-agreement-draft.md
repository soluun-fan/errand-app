# Guigere task provider agreement draft

**For Mongolian legal and accounting review. Not effective and not available for acceptance.**
Prepared 2026-11-22. This proposal must be reconciled with the actual service and mandatory law
before use. Bracketed fields and the payment schedule must be completed before signature.

## Parties and documents

Guigere, operated by **[registered legal name, entity type, registration and tax numbers,
address]**, represented by **[authorised signatory]**, engages **[provider's verified legal
name, necessary identification and contact details]** to perform separately agreed tasks.

This agreement has version **[version]**, effective **[date]**. Each accepted
[job order](work-order-draft.md) identifies the result, fee, expenses, timing and evidence for
that task. Both parties receive a copy of this agreement, the accepted order and later agreed
revisions. Mandatory law prevails; a job order can vary commercial details only within this
agreement and the law.

## Choosing and performing tasks

The provider chooses whether and when to make themselves available and may decline a new offer
without a penalty for declining. No minimum shifts, guaranteed work or exclusivity is created
by this proposal. The provider may work for others. Once an order is accepted and its stated
start conditions are met, the provider is responsible for the agreed result and timely updates.

The provider chooses how to perform the work, subject to the agreed result, access constraints,
safety requirements and law. No additional tasks are implied by a general category name.
Questions, material delays, unsuitable conditions and missing equipment must be raised promptly.
Another person must not use the provider's identity or enter the customer's premises as an
unapproved substitute; any substitution must follow a disclosed, lawful verification process.

The parties intend a task service relationship only where the facts support it. This agreement
does not waive employment rights, mandatory social insurance, minimum remuneration or other
protections. Guigere must use an appropriate employment arrangement if the actual relationship
requires one; changing terminology or limiting the number of jobs is not a substitute.

## Scope changes and safety

The order states what is included and excluded, the locations, agreed time window, equipment,
lifting limits and other material conditions. A changed result, additional stop, increased
expense limit or additional time needs an agreed order revision before the extra work starts.

The provider may stop unsafe or unlawful work and report the reason without being required to
continue for the sake of ratings or payment. The parties will arrange safe handover of goods
and resolve completed work and approved expenses under the cancellation process. A technical
outage must have a support route **[contact and hours]**.

## Fees and expenses

Each order must show the gross task fee, any disclosed platform deduction, legally required
withholding and the expected net service fee. Guigere must disclose the basis for any deduction
and provide a statement. No unspecified fine or unilateral retrospective fee is authorised.
Required tax and social-insurance registrations, contributions, deductions and reports remain
the responsibility of whichever party the law assigns them to. Attach the accountant-approved
schedule; do not assume the provider alone bears these obligations.

Purchase money is separate from the service fee. The order must state whether the provider
advances funds, the authorised limit, acceptable receipts, approved substitutions and the
reimbursement deadline. The provider need not finance a purchase they have not agreed to fund.
Amounts over the authorised limit require approval before purchase. Return unused funds or
goods through the stated, traceable process.

## Completion and payment

The provider submits the stated completion evidence. Guigere reviews performance with the
customer against the accepted scope. A complaint must identify the disputed part and supporting
facts; unrelated requirements cannot be added after completion.

Before adoption, insert an approved schedule specifying **[review duration and start event]**,
**[what happens at expiry]**, **[date or calculable deadline when the fee becomes due]**,
**[latest transfer deadline]**, and **[bank rejection and outage escalation]**. A customer's
silence or payment-provider delay must not create indefinite uncertainty about the provider's
entitlement. The currently implemented request queue does not by itself satisfy this schedule.

Guigere owes payments under the agreed order and applicable law. A customer refund or chargeback
does not automatically cancel the provider's earned fee. Pay to the provider's verified account;
explain a failed transfer and preserve the obligation until it is resolved. Supply a statement
separating task fees, reimbursements, deductions and transfers. Earnings are not transferable or
spendable within the app.

## Cancellation, problems and complaints

The order must disclose the consequences of customer cancellation, provider withdrawal,
no access, an incorrect description and unsafe conditions. Agree how completed work, reasonable
approved expenses and any cancellation compensation are assessed. There is no blanket forfeiture
of all earnings and no automatic deduction from unrelated tasks under this proposal.

Either party may submit evidence and receive a reasoned decision. State **[initial response
deadline]**, **[decision deadline]** and **[independent escalation route and deadline]** in the
approved schedule. Release undisputed amounts when legally required. Internal review must not
remove access to competent courts or other statutory remedies. Technical support for partial
resolution must be added before promising that feature in the app.

## Privacy and location

Collect only information needed for identity, eligibility, performance, payment, safety and
lawful records. Explain its recipients, retention and access rights in the privacy notice.
Keep customer addresses, contacts, access codes and photographs confidential; do not reuse them
for unrelated purposes. Evidence must avoid unnecessary faces, children and private documents.

Explain dispatch location separately from active-job sharing. The provider controls active-job
sharing; show who can see it, when it stops and how to disable it. Do not monitor off-duty
movement. Identify any necessary retention and support access explicitly. The web app cannot
promise continuous location while closed or the device is locked.

## Responsibility and ending the relationship

Guigere remains responsible for its service promises to the customer. The provider is responsible
for performing the agreed task with appropriate care. Counsel must set a proportionate,
enforceable allocation of loss and any insurance requirements for each permitted task type;
this draft contains no unlimited indemnity or exclusion of mandatory liability.

Either party may end future participation with **[notice]**; immediate restriction may be
necessary for a documented safety or fraud concern, with a way to respond and appeal. Existing
jobs need a safe resolution. Ending access does not erase earned payments, claims or legally
required recordkeeping. Changes apply prospectively after proper notice and acceptance where
required; an accepted job must not silently acquire new financial terms.

## Acceptance record

Record the parties, document version and full content, acceptance time, order versions and
agreed payment schedule. Provide downloadable copies. Complete **[governing law, competent
forum, controlling language and notice contacts]** with Mongolian counsel. Guigere and the
provider accept only after all required fields and review decisions are final.
