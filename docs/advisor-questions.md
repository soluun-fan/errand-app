# Questions for our accountant and lawyer (2026-11-25)

Background: Guigere sells small errands as a **managed service**. The customer pays us the full amount up front through wire.mn (QPay). After the customer confirms the job, we pay the helper the price they agreed. The helper keeps 100% of that price. The customer also pays a service fee of 10% of the price, at least 500₮ and at most 5,000₮. If the job includes shopping, the customer prepays the shopping money and we pass it through at cost. Helpers are independent contractors: they choose which jobs to take and negotiate their own price.

## For the accountant

1. **e-barimt.** Because we sell the service, do we issue the e-barimt for the **whole amount** the customer pays (helper's price plus our fee), or only for our fee? How do we handle the shopping money?
2. **VAT registration.** Is it measured on our fees or on the full turnover? At what monthly volume do we cross the threshold?
3. **Helper income tax.** Do we withhold personal income tax when we pay helpers, report the payments, or leave it to the helper? Is there a simplified regime for individuals doing occasional work?
4. **Payout fees.** We absorb Golomt's transfer fee on helper payouts. How should that be booked?

## For the lawyer

5. **Contractor status.**
   - About 100,000 Mongolians now work through apps such as UBCab, Toki and Shoppy.
   - A 2026 study argues platform workers should be presumed employees.
   - Labour-law penalties were revised in July 2026.
   - Question: are our terms and design enough to keep helpers clearly independent? Those terms are: free choice of jobs, no penalty for declining, own price, no exclusivity, no dress or behaviour orders.
   - What should we avoid doing?
6. **Social insurance from 1 Jan 2027.** The amendments allow flexible contributions for people on work contracts. Do we have any duty to withhold, report or notify? Could we legally offer helpers an easy way to pay voluntarily?
7. **Minimum wage.** From 1 Jan 2027 it is 1,000,000₮/month and 5,952.30₮/hour. Does it apply to contractor hourly floors at all? We have set day jobs to at least 6,000₮/h anyway.
8. **Pausing accounts.**
   - Ops can now pause an account for 7 or 30 days, for example when both sides paid outside the app.
   - A paused helper gets no new jobs; a paused customer cannot post.
   - What clause should the next version of the terms contain? Is notice or an appeal step required?
9. **Personal data (law in force since May 2022).**
   - Is our consent wording enough for: ID checks and selfies; chats kept for 90 days; live location during a job?
   - May Ops read a chat when someone files a report?
   - What exactly may we say about monitoring?
10. **Showing helper gender or age range.** If we offer it, it would be opt-in by the helper and shown only to signed-in customers. Is that allowed? Could a customer's filter on it count as discrimination?
11. **Off-app payment rule.** Our terms will say that paying outside the app can lead to both accounts being paused. Is that enforceable, and how should it be worded?
